<section class="description">

AutoFeeds brings the web to your server. Subscribe to RSS, Atom, and JSON Feed formats covering everything from YouTube channels, Subreddits, and Bluesky profiles to podcasts, blogs, and beyond. Be notified of new content directly in a channel of your choice, formatted just the way you like it.

<div class="feature-grid feeds">

<div class="feature">
<h2>Every Feed Imaginable</h2>
<p>Supports RSS, Atom, and JSON Feed formats. Subscribe to GitHub repos, Twitch streams, Mastodon accounts, Steam updates, podcasts, blogs, sports scores, and much more with just a link.</p>
</div>

<div class="feature">
<h2>Fabulous Filtering</h2>
<p>Set per-feed rules to block feed items which match certain values. Keep the sponsor slots or undesired posts out of your server.</p>
</div>

<div class="feature">
<h2>Tailored Templates</h2>
<p>Use per-feed templates with variables like <code>{title}</code>, <code>{author}</code>, and <code>{link}</code> to make every post feel right at home. Display new items as <em>you</em> want.</p>
</div>

<div class="feature">
<h2>Polite & Punctual</h2>
<p>Feeds are checked reguarly for new content and the bot adheres to best practices to be a good citizen of the web. No floods, no pestered operators.</p>
</div>
