<section class="description">

AutoDice is a fully-featured dice bot for Stoat, perfect for table-top role-playing games and anything else that calls for a roll. Whether slaying dragons or settling whose turn it is to do this dishes, AutoDice has your rolls covered with intuitive syntax and rich mechanics to cover every case.

<div class="feature-grid dice">

<div class="feature">
<h2>Simple Syntax</h2>
<p>Roll anything from a single <code>d6</code> to five sets of ten <code>d20</code>s, add arithmetic with <code>+</code>, <code>-</code>, <code>*</code>, and <code>/</code>, and label rolls with descriptive text.</p>
</div>

<div class="feature">
<h2>TTRPG Mechanics</h2>
<p>Keep or drop the highest and lowest dice, explode on max rolls, reroll poor results, and count successes and failures. Chain modifiers for even the most complex campaigns.</p>
</div>

<div class="feature">
<h2>Special Dice</h2>
<p>Roll percentile dice with <code>d%</code>, Fate/Fudge dice with <code>dF</code>, and take advantage or disadvantage with the handy <code>adv</code> and <code>dis</code> shorthands.</p>
</div>

<div class="feature">
<h2>Sneaky Rolls</h2>
<p>Prefix a roll with <code>dm</code> to get the result straight in your direct messages, away from prying party eyes.</p>
</div>

</div>
